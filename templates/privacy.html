Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and password (stored as a hashed value). We also store the expense data you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so the Service can show it back to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to operate and improve the Service: to authenticate you, display your expenses, and generate summaries and reports. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and access is restricted to you via your account credentials. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account and the data associated with it at any time. Deleting your account removes your expense history and profile information from the Service.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
